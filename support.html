Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Everything CTRCH saves — accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, bills, currencies and receipt photos — is stored locally on your iPhone. You can export a backup through the Files app at any time.

I lost my data after deleting the app. Can you help?

If you had previously exported a backup file, restoring it through the app will bring your data back. If no backup was exported, the locally stored data is removed with the app and cannot be recovered by us, because we never had access to it.

Can I use CTRCH on multiple devices?

CTRCH is designed as a single-device companion that keeps your data private. You can move data to another device by exporting a backup from one device and importing it on the other.

Does CTRCH support widgets or Apple Watch?

CTRCH focuses on the iPhone experience today. Widgets and other surfaces may be considered in future updates.

How do I report a bug?

Send an email to dalelipscomb1@gmail.com with a description, your iOS version and your iPhone model. If possible, include a screenshot or short screen-recording.
