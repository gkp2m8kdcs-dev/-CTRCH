Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the things people ask most about CTRCH.

Does CTRCH require an account?

No. CTRCH has no login, no signup and no account. All data lives privately on your device.

Does CTRCH connect to my bank?

No. CTRCH never asks for bank login details and never connects to any financial institution.

How is my data stored?

All transactions, accounts, budgets, goals and bills are stored locally on your device in JSON. You can back up and restore through the Files app at any time.

Which currencies are supported?

CTRCH supports 20+ currencies with editable reference rates so totals and charts stay accurate as rates change.
